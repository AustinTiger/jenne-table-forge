## (FUN) WILD MAGIC SURGE TABLE VOLUME 1

## AMUSING WILD MAGIC FROM THE E MIND OF FOWL DAMASCUS

Introduction: The Wild Magic Surge Table is a great tool for those sorcerers who love to introduce chaos into roleplaying. However, the table presented in the Player's Handbook has some effects that are rather boring g in my mind. So, this supplement is here to inject a bit of fun, humor, and unique roleplaying opportunities for a sorcerer and their victims...er... party members. These effects I created based on my most favored/hated wild magic sorcerer ally I ever partied with, Fowl Damascus. Enjoy!

## WILDMAGIC SURGE VOLUME1

**A note using the table:

Some players will undoubtedly seek to abuse the less lethal/dangerous nature of these surges in order to use their "Tides of Chaos" feature or similar features. It is my recommendation that in such cases have the players roll on the regular Wild Magic Surge Table in the Player's Handbook. That table has more dangerous (and also more boring) surge options that may better police abusive players.

Some players will find these options presented here to be too dangerous! If this is the case, simply don't use the effects in question or work to find a similar option that all players can agree with.

My general opinion is that some of these effects are indeed dangerous, but more than anything else they are going to create a fun, memorable experience for the party. In the end, who wouldn't want that? Also, many of these options are left intentionally vague to allow for player and DM interpretation so that these options can better fit your individual play groups.

Finally, and perhaps most importantly, all the effects here can be ended with a casting of Dispel Magic or Remove Curse.
