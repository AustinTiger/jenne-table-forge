## HOW TO USE THIS BOOK
This book is meant to help game masters run eldritch, horror, or mythos-inspired role-playing games. The tables contain weird or strange objects and situations with a hint of the 1920s and 1930s. The random tables are all presented as D100 and can be used in various ways.
Game masters can roll on the tables before sessions to add details to their campaigns or they can pick options. Another method is to have players roll during sessions as the situation demands.
The first section is Items. Here you will find Fictional Occult Book Titles, Haunted Objects, Items in a Hotel themed for the 1930s, 200 Oddities, and Real Occult Book Titles.
The Clues section contains three different random tables. The first is a large and detailed table called Dead Bodies with Clues. The next is Eldritch Clues and then lastly Random Clues. These all help GMs add mystery and complexity to campaigns quickly and creatively.
Next is the Places section. The Dreamscapes table provides bizarre and otherworldly visions or places characters can experience. The final table here is a list of Fictional New England Towns.
The Angel and Demon Names section contains the names of 400 angels and demons mentioned in history or ancient literature.
The Phobias section is a simple list with a thumbnail description of strange and odd, irrational fears.
The Cryptids section provides a list of the names of creatures that live at the edge of reality yet firmly in our imaginations.
Next up is the People section. There are tables of Alchemists, Detective Names, Fictional Occult Scholar Names, and Occultists. Game masters always need names. Having a setting appropriate name on hand enhances the realism of RPG sessions.
The Eldritch Story Hooks section gives GMs the seeds to build sessions and campaigns. If you need ideas fast or your creative well is running dry, use this table. Also, this table could be used as rumors the characters encounter during the course of their other investigations.
The book is capped offwith a list of H.P. Lovecraft Stories. Game masters may wish to do further research into the author who filled us all with a sense of cosmic horror.
May you enjoy your travels in lands of mystery and even dread because of the random tables you find here.
