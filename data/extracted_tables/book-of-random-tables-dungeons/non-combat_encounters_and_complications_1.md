## Non-Combat Encounters and Complications
1. An animated mop and bucket eagerly clean the floor.
2. A young girl sits in the corner weeping.
3. Invisible rats scurry around the party.
4. A cat caught in an animal trap is surrounded by her mewing kittens.
5. A stone idol hangs from the ceiling by ropes, it appears to be magical.
6. A huge root grown through the wall blocks the way.
7. Small woodland creatures are making a dress out of scraps of cloth on a dressmaker's mannequin.
8. The passage is flooded with three feet of stagnant water.
9. An animated disembodied hand is gathering pebbles in a corner.
10. The floor is hot to the touch.
11. Tiny people only a few inches tall have built an elaborate walled city out of stone debris and small pieces of wood.
12. Water ominously drips from the ceiling.
13. Books fly around the room like birds.
14. The floor is coated in oil.
15. The ceiling looks like the night sky full of stars but is in fact bioluminescent insects.
16. Barely audible whispers seem to follow the party.
17. The walls are beautifully painted depicting a mountain range. Upon examination, a coded message is written within the lines and strokes.
18. A ghost appears and seems to beckon the party into a certain room.
19. An illusion of a woman appears and tells the story of an ancient kingdom and how it fell.
20. Stepping on the floor causes loud creaking and cracking sounds.
21. A gnome asks for help lifting a large stone off   the floor. He says he's collecting insect samples.
22. A thin layer of ice covers the entire floor.
23. A stone gargoyle tells the party riddles and warns them they may be turned to stone.
24. The ceiling seems to radiate cold.
25. Shouts come from the other side of the wall. It seems like someone has been sealed into the wall.
26. Vines grow down the front of the ceiling and they are so thick they need to be hacked through.
27. A ghost of a child asks the party to read a bedtime story.
28. The room or passage is filled with a thick, dreadful fog.
29. A pile of bones that laughs as the party passes.
30. Tremors shake the dungeon every few minutes showering the party with dust.
31. Moss on the floor grips the party's feet and slows them.
32. An old woman sits at a spinning wheel, and she asks the party if they have any thread.
33. A figure wanders through the dungeon muttering it's lost, but it appears not to be able to see the party.
34. A man locked in a cage begs to be set free.
35. One room appears to be an enchanted fairy forest complete with trees, flowers, butterflies, and more.
36. The party stumbles upon a deserted camp yet there is a fire going and food cooking.
37. Eyes embedded in the wall watch the party as they walk along.
38. Scratching sounds seem to indicate something or someone is digging on the other side of the wall.
39. In one room a circle of statues stands, with each one holding a globe. However, one statue is missing a globe.
40. A dog whines and digs at something in the corner of the room.
