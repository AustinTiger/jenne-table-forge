## Cursed Items
1. The Twin Daggers - Two sentient daggers that hate each other
2. Amulet of Existence - This amulet exists
3. Glove of Zero Division - Divides by zero; destroys reality
4. Holding of Bags- A small bag that can magic hold other bags
5. Necklace of Mind Reading - Anyone within a 30 feet radius can read the wearer's mind
6. Ring of Teleportation - The ring teleports anywhere the wearer wills; the wearer does not
7. Sword of Pain - Screams in agony whenever it hits something
8. Rock of Gravity Detection - Hold out and release, if it falls gravity is working
9. Axe of Scents - Does no damage; makes the target smell bad
10. Ring of Disappearance - The ring disappears when equipped
11. Cloak of Tongues - The wearer can taste anything within a 30-yard radius
12. Mace of Healing - Heals the target up to 1d6 health; also does 1d6 damage
13. Boots of Waterwalking - The wearer can walk on shallow water of 3 inches depth or less
14. Ring of Personal Invisibility - The wearer is invisible only to themselves
15. Sword of Offense - A talking sword that insults the enemy, inflicting 3d6 emotional damage
16. Cloak of the Weather - A talking cloak that constantly complains about the weather
17. Warhammer of Chicken Detection - Glows bright yellow when there is a chicken within 30 feet
18. Necklace of Mind Control - Allows the wearer to control their own mind
19. Ring of Confident Invisibility - The wearer is convinced they are completely invisible, they are not (itself)
20. Wand of Detect Magic Item - Detects the nearest magic item
21. Goggles of Darkvision - Makes everything appear darker
22. Shield of Protection - In combat, it uses the bearer's body to shield itself from harm
23. Deck of Risk - The owner of this deck of cards makes unwisely large bets while playing cards
24. Gloves of Pickpocketing - Allows others to pickpocket the wearer easier
25. Ring of Inn Visibility - Shows the wearer where all inns are within a 300-foot radius
26. Bagpipes of Invisibility - The wielder is invisible as long as they are playing the bagpipes
27. Singing Frog - Sings when alone with its owner; croaks like a normal frog when anyone else is near
28. Ring of the Ring - Allows the wearer to equip one more ring than they otherwise could
29. Sword of Lightning - 5% chance for the wielder to be struck by lightning when used
30. Staff of Judgment - Judgmental talking staff ; constant passive-aggressive comments
31. Locket of Impression - The wearer has a vague impression that something isn't right singing
32. Necklace of the Opera - Causes the wearer to communicate only by operatic
33. Shield of Overconfidence - Compels the bearer to take unnecessary risks
34. Ring of Attraction - Attracts bees and hornets
35. Candle of Flame Resistance - It's a fireproof candle
36. Hat of Marvel - Reduces the wearer's intelligence to 3, but convinces others the wearer is a genius
37. Helm of Relentless Growth - Causes the wearer's hair to grow so fast that it pushes the helm off
38. Amulet of Pitch - Causes the wearer's voice to be two octaves higher
39. Bag of Spewing - A magic bag that shoots out items that are mentioned in a conversation
40. Boots of Invisibility - Turns the wearer's feet invisible (the boots do not become invisible)
41. Dagger of Volume - The wielder can only communicate by loudly shrieking
42. Boots of Feather Falling - A ton of feathers fall on the wearer every time they step outside
43. Emerald of Giving - Compels the wearer to give away all of their wealth until only the emerald left
44. Sword of Dancing - The wielder dances uncontrollably when in combat
45. Gauntlets of Hilarity - Causes the wearer to giggle uncontrollably at inappropriate times
46. Mask of Near-Perfect Disguise - Disguises the wearer as a near-perfect imitation of themselves
47. Hat of Luck Reversal - The wearer is unlucky, prone to ridiculous scenarios and sticky situations
48. Vest of Cursed Hearing - Wearer can hear everything said within 100' but can't comprehend it useful
49. Pants of Cursed Knowledge - Wearer learns many new things none of them
