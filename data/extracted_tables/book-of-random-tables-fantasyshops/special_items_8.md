## Special Items
The shop has a number of unique items on hand.
1. Bloodhound pups (1D8, advantage to investigation checks when used to track a single living being)
2. Parrot (can imitate 1D4 voices, only 1D6 phrases per voice)
3. Horse (advantage to animal handling checks while riding it)
4. Rat (advantage to intimidation checks when sitting on the owner's shoulder)
