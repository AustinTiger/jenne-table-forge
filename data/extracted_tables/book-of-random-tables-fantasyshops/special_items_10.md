## Special Items
The shop has 1D4 unique items on hand.
1. Lyre of Soothing (advantage to performance checks while being played)
2. Pan Flute of Calling (when played in the forest will call 1D8 harmless forest animals)
3. The Emperor's Fiddle (rumored to have onace belonged to an ancient emperor)
4. The Scarlet Lute (advantage to investigation checks while being played)
