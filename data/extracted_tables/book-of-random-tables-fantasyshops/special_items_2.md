## Special Items
The shop has 1D4 unique items on hand.
1. Rose-colored  Reading  Glasses  (allows the wearer to read 1D4 languages they do not know)
2. The  Wizard's  Companion  (advantage to intelligent checks while holding this book in both hands)
3. Reading Lamp of Gazing (light from the lamp reveals any hidden writing)
4. The Coursing Stream (rare book on sale for 100gp but is actually worth 1500gp)
