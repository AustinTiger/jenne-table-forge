## Thieves Guild Quests
1. A member of the guild has been kidnapped and is being held hostage You are sent to pay the ransom and to deliver a little surprise if you desire.
2. Set fire to the shop of an uncooperative shopkeeper.
3. Replace a letter being delivered to the king with a fake one.
4. Steal the queen's signet ring so the leader of the guild can use it. Then return it unnoticed.
5. You are sent to the poor quarter of town to recruit orphans into the guild.
6. A wealthy dwarf noble has traveled to the city and established himself in the best rooms at the inn. Pay his rooms a visit and take a small 'toll.'
7. Retrieve a special order short sword from the blacksmith and bring it to the guild leader.
8. Steal a dungeon map from the captain of the guard.
9. Steal a certain potion from the crazy old wizard who lives at the edge of town.
10. Steal a bottle of wine from the inn.
11. Steal a brooch from a noble's manor house.
12. You are sent to explore some ruins that have been recently discovered on a hill outside of town.
13. Steal a small gold idol from a ship moored in port
14. Steal an ancient sword from the town's lorekeeper.
15. Pickpocket three people before noon.
16. Steal the ivory key that a certain noblewoman always wears around her neck. She must not be harmed.
17. One of your fellow members was caught in the act. He is now in the town dungeon. Liberate your comrade.
18. Someone is killing members of the guild. Investigate before you become a victim.
19. Take the ashes of a deceased member of the guild up to the pool near the waterfall just north of town and sprinkle them there.
20. Steal the alarm horn from the main gate tower of the city.
21. Find out who stole a noble's painting. It was not the work of the guild.
22. Some new players moved into town and set up a casino operation. It's time for the guild to get a piece of the action.
23. A  noble  has  paid  a  great  sum  for  two  elephant  tusks  to  be  mounted  in  his  palace.  An interested party is willing to pay even more to have the guild steal them.
24. Steal several ingredients from the herbalist shop and bring them to the old healer that works for the guild.
25. The three great noble houses of the city each possess a ruby as a symbol of their power. The guild wants these stones.
26. Some adventurer at the inn has been bragging about how he has a treasure map. Relieve him of his property.
27. Some fishermen haven't paid their dues. Go to their cabins and find any money they are hiding.
28. Poison the drink of a certain nobleman. He only drinks a special vintage of wine that can only be found own wine cellar.
29. A String of burglaries in the upper class side of town has everyone on edge. Nobody in the guild knows or will confess to the crimes. Investigate and find the burglar.
30. Steal a rowboat and bring it to a certain spot that the guild leader told you about.
31. Steal a painting from a noble's mansion and replace it with a fake.
32. Steal some honey from a noble's beehives.
33. A new merchant has defied the guild. Demonstrate to him why it is better to cooperate.
34. Steal an opal from the second story bedroom of a noble.
35. Steal a rare bottle of wine from a noble.
36. Assassinate an uncooperative shopkeeper and make it public.
37. Steal a certain coin from a noble's coin collection
38. Track down a rogue guild member and bring them alive to the guild leader.
39. An exotic vessel has arrived from a faraway land. The captain and crew brag that they have - exotic goods worth many gold pieces. Sneak on board and take an inventory.
40. Escort the daughter of a high ranking guild member to the next town.
41. Bring 100 gold pieces to the guild leader no questions asked.
42. Sneak into the harbor master's office and copy a ledger about arriving and departing ships.
43. Conduct a series of small raids on a rival guild.
44. A party of adventurers need a burglar. If you're up for some freelance work that is.
45. Steal a painting from a noble's private collection
46. Steal a statue from a noble's mansion.
47. A ship's captain doublecrossed the guild. Set fire to his ship.
48. Steal a bracelet that belongs to a noble.
49. Steel a noble's famous wine recipe.
50. Follow a certain noble for three days and report all of his movements to the guild leader.
