## How to Use this Book
Welcome to the second book in my series of random 1D100 tables.
It is my firm conviction based on decades of game mastering that random tables are one of the most important tools in a GM's kit.
Random tables cut session prep time and provide sparks of creativity and randomness that keep sessions and GMs fresh.
Simply roll on the random table that best fits your game situation and allow the story to emerge.
The tables in this  book  are  divided  into  five  categories:  Names,  Dungeons,  Items  &amp; Things, People, and Towns.
Since the first book in the series has 600 names for humans, there are 300 names for creatures in this book. Never have a goblin named 'Bob' again, unless it fits your story.
Rooms in dungeons are always tricky. Players want to search every inch, but as a game master, you don't want to keep giving out treasure. The four dungeon room tables give interesting items for the characters to find without enriching them.
The  item  tables  provide  a  wide  variety  of  items  from  mundane  tools  to  exciting adventure hooks.
The tables organized under the People section offer quick non-player characters, jobs, encounters, and more.
Coming up with a realistic town name after much thought is a challenge. Coming up with one on the fly is insanely difficult. The 500 town names in the book are drawn from villages in Great Britain that no longer exist, so they sound very convincing.
I hope you enjoy the random tables, and I wish you many fruitful adventures.
Matt Davids 5/1/2018
