## Ways to Space
1. In a faraway land, a great work has been undertaken. Thousands labor under the direction of a wealthy queen and her mad engineer. The massive building project is said to be a grappling hook that can reach the moon.
2. The great sky sturgeon swim the skies swinging low only on rare occasions. It is said that if one were to swallow a person, it would carry them to the stars.
3. The party stumbles upon an old woman weaving a gigantic basket. She says that anyone who steps into the basket will be transported to realms beyond this world. However, she needs a few other things before the basket is finished.
4. A spectral ferryman carries the party across a river. However, they don't arrive on the other side of the river. Instead, they arrive in space.
5. A roc ferries willing riders to space.
6. An ancient book of riddles is rumored to contain an incantation that will bear the reciter to space.
7. It is said an ancient king kept winged armor that could carry him to the stars. Sadly, his kingdom now lays in ruins. Perhaps the armor rests under the rubble.
8. It is said that one of the towers of the old palace is actually a spaceship. If the party could determine which one it is, they may be able to take it to space.
9. A village of gnomes uses gliders to navigate a powerful wind current to reach space.
10. A village of gnomes built a giant projectile cannon. They intend to launch a group of adventurers into space if only they can find volunteers.
11. Giant bubbles rise along the coastline of a certain sea. It is said that if a person steps into a bubble, they are carried to space.
12. At the bottom of a dungeon, a skeleton guarding a bubbling pool tells the party if they bathe in it, they will be taken to the space beyond.
13. In a dungeon, the party finds a selection of ancient masks. As the party learns about the masks, it becomes clear that if they put on certain masks they will be transported to the stars.
14. A mouse offers to take the party to space in its spaceship.
15. A gnome inventor has created several pairs of winged shoes. He tells the party they can fly to space. Just don't fly too close to the sun.
16. A crazy gnome inventor tells the party that a giant spiderweb can be used like a slingshot as a means of travel. The party is offered gold to help test it, but instead of traveling on earth, they are thrown all the way into space.
17. An elf inventor has attached thousands of balloons to her house. Regardless of why the party visits her, at that moment, the house takes flight and journeys to space.
18. While traveling the party is hit by a tornado. When they come to, they are in space.
19. Once every one hundred years, a huge geyser launches a stream of water into space. It is said that intrepid souls have ridden the spout to the heavens.
20. A gnome village has constructed a giant trebuchet. They claim it can launch people to the realms beyond the world.
21. The party is told to take a gold coin to the old man who lives in the forest. The old man tells them to take a fish to a young woman who lives on the docks. The young woman tells the party to swim back and forth across the bay four times. When they return to her she tells them to take a chicken to the weaver woman. The weaver woman gives them a swatch of cloth with a strange design and tells them to take it to the king's wizard. The wizard puts the cloth in a machine and pulls a lever. The party is transported to space.
22. A secluded wizard's tower is said to rise to the heavens every six months taking the occupants to space.
23. A village of wood elves has constructed a gigantic bow with the ability to shoot people into space.
24. Once every year the great firebird rises up to the heavens. Brave souls occasionally hitch a ride.
25. A mischievous pixie sparkles the party with pixie dust. They soon discover that they are now in space.
26. The shadow of a fell beast falls over the party and they find themselves in space.
27. A magical gateway deep in a dungeon leads to space.
28. A wise woman tells the party if they want to reach the space beyond, they should jump off   a certain high cliff    in a foreign land. She tells them not to be afraid.
29. The party finds an old beggar dying alongside the road. After caring for him, he gives them a key made of an unknown metal. The man dies and the key begins to always 'point' in the same direction. If the party follows that direction, they will find a spaceship.
30. A giant is known to throw his spear beyond the bonds of earth. Some say people could ride the spear and journey to space with it.
31. The oldtimers say the mound outside of town covers a 'sky chariot' that fell from the heavens long ago. Is anyone brave enough to find out the truth?
32. Taking a boat over a waterfall on a river leads to space.
33. If you speak the true name of a star you will be transported to it. It is said a wizard has gathered at least two true names of stars which he keeps hidden away in his secret tower.
34. A certain sentient species of giant bats are said to take people into space. However, many don't believe the tales anymore.
35. The whirling vortex in the center of a deep dungeon leads to space.
36. Staring at a painting of a spacescape in a noble's house causes the party to be transported to space.
37. As the party reaches the final room in a dungeon, they realize they have been exploring a giant spaceship. Can they learn how to control it?
38. A crazy inventor claims to have created a paste that peels the gravity off   of objects. He wishes to travel to space and needs help.
39. While searching a ruin, the party finds a room with broomsticks in it. There is the same number of brooms as party members. The party can tell they are flying brooms. Once outside, if the party rides the broomsticks, they are taken to space.
40. Rumor tells of a beanstalk grown so tall it reaches to the stars. Perhaps brave souls could climb it?
41. A unicorn offers to transport the party to space.
42. At the bottom of a deep dungeon, it is rumored that there is a magic door that leads to the stars.
43. An ancient massive albatross offers to carry the party to the space beyond.
44. While traveling, a god reaches down and scoops the party up and deposits them into space.
45. While sailing the party's vessel is caught up on a huge wave that carries the entire ship into space.
46. Legends say a wizard holds a magic ring in a secret library. The ring can transport people to space, or at least that is what the tales say.
47. An ancient gate stands abandoned. It is said riddles were used to take wizards and warriors to the stars. The party can search for clues around the gate or in libraries for the riddles.
48. A great winged book has been seen flying over the city in the past weeks. The city leadership would like someone to look into it. Once the party captures it, it flies them to space.
49. A gnome inventor has created a gun that shoots a rope with a hook. He's looking for help to shoot a shadow he sees pass over his house every other week or so at night. When they shoot the gun, the hook catches on to a spaceship and they are carried into space.
50. A large stone circle dug up in the desert proves to be a gateway to the heavens.
51. The giant whales of the deep are said to travel through unknown and mysterious routes to visit their heavenly brethren once every twenty years or so. Friends of the whales are often invited along for the journey.
52. The old tales tell of a song that if played underneath a starry sky, the performer and the audience are transported to space.
53. While exploring a dungeon, the party encounters an archway. It tells them that if they step through they will be taken to the realms of the stars.
54. A great winged horse offers the party a ride to space.
55. While the party is riding in a wagon, a giant mule kicks them and the wagon into space.
56. While traveling, the party comes across a field with dandelions taller than buildings. Grabbing onto a pappus will lead to the individual or group being carried to space.
57. A gnome inventor tricks the party into stepping into the machine he created. They are transported to space.
58. After helping an old woman, she gives the party a golden egg. She tells them when they are alone in the forest, to throw it over one of their shoulders and keep walking. When the party does so, they find themselves in space.
59. It is said that in a faraway land, the trees grow so tall that the elves who live among them throw ropes up to space and explore the worlds beyond.
60. Tales tell of a portal behind a waterfall deep in the forest that leads to space.
61. The old ravens tell of a sky path where those without wings can walk to the heavens.
62. The party discovers two enormous eagles harnessed to a wagon. When they climb into the wagon the eagles fly them to space.
63. A group of elven scientists has built a flying machine. It resembles a great hawk, and they are looking for a hardy crew. However, the flight doesn't go exactly right because everyone ends up in space.
64. A rainbow bridge leads to space, if you are brave enough to cross it.
65. The hollow trunk of a large fallen tree leads to a portal to space.
66. A dense fog surrounds the party and as they emerge they find themselves in space.
67. A massive fissure spews out titanic gusts of wind into the air continually. A crazy inventor believes that, with a flying device and the gusts, a person could reach space.
68. A giant winged bear offers to carry those who wish up to space. However, some suggest the cost may be high.
69. A wizard gives the party a magic rope in return for them performing a favor. He tells them the rope could be used to lasso a shooting star. Once the party secures the star, they are pulled along for a ride into space.
70. Being struck by lightning on the summit of a certain mountain takes the party into space.
71. Every spring a huge ship with wings descends from the heavens on the fields of certain kingdoms and drops seeds. It is said when the ship leaves there are always people missing.
72. A large ship fashioned after a swan swoops down from the heavens and scoops up the party.
73. The locals say if someone jumps into the old well they are transported to space.
74. A dragon double-crosses the party and sends them to space.
75. A fireball descending from the heavens suddenly stops, changes course, and approaches the party. It asks them if they would like a ride to space.
76. While wandering in the forest, beams begin piercing down through the canopy. When one touches the party, it is taken into space.
77. A gnome inventor created a massive hot air balloon. Instead of flying around the world, it takes the party to space.
78. A phantom coachman offers the party a ride to space.
79. It is said that there is a part of the ocean where storms never end. Waterspouts wander to and fro on the surface of the waters. If a ship manages to sail into one, it will be thrust up into space.
80. A humongous whirlpool is believed to transport ships to the wild realms of other worlds.
81. Legends tell of a rope that lowers from the heavens every dozen years or so near a small village for a single day. The stories also say people have climbed it to the stars.
82. Opening a magic book transports the party to space.
83. It is said that a group of celestial beings tends a strange capsule in a hidden fortress The capsule is said to travel to the stars.
84. A wizard has created a magical thread made from the silk of baby spiders. The silk can be thrown into the air, and an individual can be 'ballooned' into space.
85. The first overnight rest after the party speaks with an old woman along the road, they find themselves in space.
86. Tales speak of a massive feather kept at the heart of an ancient treasure vault. It is said that if it is brought into the open air, the feather will carry adventurers to space.
87. A winged lion offers to transport the party to space.
88. On a certain mountain, a cloud descends to whoever climbs to the summit. If the party steps on the cloud, it rises up to space.
89. The locals say a farmer dug a hole in his field, and instead of having a bottom it actually leads to space.
90. In a far-offcorner of the sea, a glowing globe arises out of the water every sunset and ascends to the heavens. Old sailor tales speak of people riding the globe up to the stars.
91. The party encounters a strange group of vaguely humanoid beings in the woods. They are willing to take the party to space for more adventures.
92. As the party observes a planetary alignment, they are transported to space.
93. A flying carpet transports the party into space.
94. A dragon offers the party a ride over mountains but instead, he takes them to space.
95. A massive kaleidoscope of butterflies surrounds the party and carries them to space.
96. Legends tell of a stairway on top of a mountain that leads to space and the wild realms beyond. The legends also say boundless treasure awaits those who climb the stairs.
97. Sailing a ship into a sea cavern leads to space.
98. A wizard has opened a portal to space and is looking for adventurers to travel with him.
99. The chariot of the sun rests every night on an island in the middle of the ocean. It is said brave souls can barter passage to the heavens from the driver.
100. While traveling on a stormy day the party is struck by lightning and they find themselves in space.
