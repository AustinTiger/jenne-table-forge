## How to Use this Book

A game master or dungeon master can never have too many random tables. Random tables fi  ll the gaps and supply details that are too tedious to create beforehand.

The tables in this book operate exactly like the tables in other books in the Books of Random Tables series. GMs and DMs can roll percentile dice on the tables or they can have their players roll at appropriate times.

Never tell a player they fi  nd nothing in a room and never miss an opportunity to add layers of complexity to your campaign. Random tables are a dungeon master's best friend.

The fi  rst section of tables in this book is Items &amp; Things. These are D100 tables of common and unique items that are arranged either by location or item type. Included are Book Titles, Items in a Cargo Hold, Items in a Wizard's Lab, and more.

The next section is Places &amp; Locations. Adventurers need places to adventure. These two tables provide worlds of opportunity for fun adventures. Tourist Traps is an entertaining addition to fantasy space settings and, of course, Worlds and Places are a must.

Discovering  new  lifeforms  is  one  of  the  main  appeals  of  space  settings. The Alien Animals section gives game masters interesting creatures for their players' characters to encounter.

Getting existing fantasy characters into space can be challenging. The Ways to Space section makes sure characters can be taken into space in creative and memorable ways.

The  Rumors  section  gives  dungeon  masters  the  ability  to  add  side  quests  or  simply  add dynamism  to  games  by  creating  the  feeling  that  other  things  are  happening  outside  the characters' immediate surroundings.

Game masters always need names and lots of them. The Names section ensures that GMs and DMs will never get caught without a setting-appropriate name. There are Insectoid Names, Mechanical Being Names, Ooze Names, Ship Names, Space Clown Names, and World Names.

I have created these tables to help game masters run the best game possible. It is my hope that they will contribute to a vast array of adventures in astral and celestial seas.
